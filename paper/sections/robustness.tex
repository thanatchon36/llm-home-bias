\section{Robustness}\label{sec:robust}

Table~\ref{tab:robust} reports $\beta$ under each pre-registered check. The negative sign survives keeping only replies that sum to exactly 100 (\rbExact{}\,pp), the original 95--105 rule (\rbOrigRule{}\,pp, with \nOrigRuleExcluded{} models excluded), English prompts only (\rbEnglish{}\,pp), unspecified investors only (\rbUnspec{}\,pp) and a log-ratio outcome (\rbLogRatio{} log points). Leaving out one model at a time gives \lomoMin{} to \lomoMax{}\,pp; the estimate loses significance without \lomoInsigModels{}, so the negative sign rests on a few models. On the 60-prompt robustness subset, greedy decoding gives \rbGreedySixty{}\,pp, temperature-0.7 sampling with two seeds \rbTemp{}\,pp (95\% CI \rbTempLo{} to \rbTempHi{}) and dropping the system prompt \rbNosys{}\,pp (95\% CI \rbNosysLo{} to \rbNosysHi{}). The post-hoc specification that allows each model its own dispersion around equal weight gives \betaPosthocDisp{}\,pp (95\% CI \betaPosthocDispLo{} to \betaPosthocDispHi{}). Across every specification, including the leave-one-out fits and the post-hoc check, the highest upper bound of any 95\% CI is \rbHiMax{}\,pp: a positive home tilt of one percentage point or more is excluded everywhere.
